OBC Reference Guides
Cottage & Rural Plumbing
  • Suction Lift Limits: Standard jet pumps max out at 25 ft of vertical lift. Exceeding this requires a submersible pump.
  • Winterization Grading: Seasonal cottage supply lines must be graded continuously to a low-point drain valve to ensure complete gravity drain-down.
  • Frost Protection (Bedrock): Pipes unable to reach the frost line must be protected using closed-cell insulation and thermostatically controlled heat trace cable.
